# Art Direction

Turn a brief into a coherent visual concept that others can execute.

## Process
1. **Interpret the brief:** objective, audience, message, tone, constraints, budget.
2. **Find the insight:** the human truth the visual idea hangs on.
3. **Write the concept** in one sentence plus a tagline-level expression.
4. **Build the visual language:** palette, light, texture, composition, typography, casting/props, motion feel.
5. **Create the moodboard** with a purpose per image (color, light, framing, texture, emotion). Remove anything that doesn't earn its place.
6. **Define rules and don'ts** so teams stay on-direction.
7. **Hand off:** shot list, layout references, asset specs.

## Concept quality tests
- Can it be explained in one sentence?
- Does it still work in black and white and at thumbnail size?
- Is it ownable, would it look wrong for a competitor?
- Does it scale across formats (still, motion, social, OOH)?

## Direction toolkit
- **Light:** hard vs soft, direction, color temperature.
- **Framing:** close, wide, symmetrical, off-center, crops.
- **Color story:** dominant, accent, avoided.
- **Texture/finish:** grain, gloss, paper, noise.
- **Tone:** playful, serious, premium, raw, warm, clinical.

## Deliverable template
Concept statement, 3 supporting principles, moodboard sections, color and type, do/don't list, sample key visual description, production notes.

Use `references/visual-styles.md` for style vocabulary.
