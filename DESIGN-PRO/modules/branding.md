# Branding

Build identities that are distinctive, flexible and consistent.

## Brand foundation (do first)
- **Purpose:** why the brand exists beyond profit.
- **Audience:** who, their context, what they value.
- **Positioning:** the one space it owns versus competitors.
- **Personality:** 3-5 traits, each with a "but not" (e.g. confident, *not* arrogant).
- **Voice:** tone range and sample lines for headline, body, error/empty states.

## Identity system
- **Logo suite:** primary, secondary/stacked, icon/mark, monochrome, reversed.
- **Clear space and minimum size** defined relative to a logo element (e.g. the x-height).
- **Color:** 1 primary, 1-2 secondary, neutrals, functional (success/warn/error). Give HEX, RGB, CMYK, and usage ratios (e.g. 60/30/10).
- **Typography:** one display + one text face (see `typography.md`).
- **Imagery and graphic devices:** photography style, illustration, patterns, icon style.
- **Applications:** stationery, social avatars, packaging, signage, digital.

## Logo craft checklist
- Works at 16px and on a billboard.
- Reads in one color and on both light and dark.
- Distinct silhouette; avoids clichés of the category.
- No dependence on gradients, effects or thin hairlines to survive.
- Test: blur it, shrink it, invert it, view it in a row of competitors.

## Deliverables
Brand strategy summary, logo files and rules, palette and type specs, a 1-2 page quick-start guide, and application mockup list.

Follow `workflows/branding-workflow.md` for the step sequence.
