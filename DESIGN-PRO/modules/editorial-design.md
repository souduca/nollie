# Editorial Design

Layout for magazines, books, reports and long-form content, where reading experience and pacing matter.

## Structure
- **Format and page size** driven by content and production.
- **Grid:** columns (2-6), margins, gutters, baseline grid shared across spreads.
- **Master pages** with running heads, folios and section markers.
- **Hierarchy:** title, deck, byline, subheads, body, pull quotes, captions, footnotes, sidebars, each as a defined style.

## Pacing
- Alternate dense and airy spreads; vary image scale.
- Open sections with a strong spread (full-bleed image, large type).
- Place pull quotes and breakers to reset attention every 1-2 pages.
- Plan a **flatplan** (page-by-page map) before detailed layout.

## Text rules
- 45-75 characters per line; body 9-11 pt print with 1.3-1.5 leading.
- Paragraph style: indent *or* space, not both.
- Hyphenation and justification tuned to avoid rivers and loose lines.
- Captions: consistent position, smaller size, credit style.

## Images
Crop to emphasize meaning, keep consistent treatment (color grade, borders), maintain 300 dpi, respect the gutter (avoid faces and key content across it).

## Reports and data
Lead with insight headlines; one chart, one message; label directly instead of legends where possible; use consistent chart colors from the palette.

## Production
Bleed, color mode, imposition, binding allowance (extra inner margin), pagination multiples of 4/8/16, proofing checklist.
