# Graphic Design

Composition for posters, print, packaging and static visuals.

## Composition
- Start with a **grid** (columns, margins, baseline). Break it deliberately, not accidentally.
- Create a **focal point**; give it size, contrast or isolation.
- Use **scale contrast**: at least a 3x jump between primary and secondary text.
- Group by **proximity**; separate groups with more space than the space inside groups.
- Align to few axes; avoid more than three alignment lines per layout.
- Let **negative space** do work; crowding signals low quality.

## Print essentials
- Resolution: 300 dpi at final size; vector for logos and type.
- Bleed 3 mm (0.125 in), safe margin 5 mm inside trim.
- Use CMYK for print, RGB for screen; check rich black (e.g. C60 M40 Y40 K100) for large areas only.
- Outline fonts or package them; export PDF/X-4 where supported.
- Specify paper, finish and spot colors early; they change the design.

## Packaging
- Design the dieline first; map front, side and top reading order.
- Required legal copy gets its own hierarchy tier, planned from the start.
- Shelf test: 2 m distance and 3 seconds to identify product, brand, variant.

## Common failures
Too many fonts, low-contrast text on images, centered everything, decorative effects covering weak hierarchy, unaligned margins, stretched or low-res images.
