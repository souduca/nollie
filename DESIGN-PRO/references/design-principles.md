# Design Principles

Universal fundamentals referenced by every module.

| Principle    | Meaning                                              | Quick test                              |
|--------------|------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------|
| Hierarchy    | Order of importance through size, weight, color, position | Squint: is the order of reading clear? |
| Contrast     | Difference that creates emphasis and legibility      | Is anything competing for first place?  |
| Balance      | Visual weight distributed (symmetric or asymmetric)  | Does the layout feel stable or tilted?  |
| Alignment    | Shared edges and axes                                | Can you draw few clean lines through it?|
| Proximity    | Related items grouped                                | Are groups obvious without borders?     |
| Repetition   | Recurring styles build unity                         | Would a stranger see these as a family? |
| White space  | Room to breathe, focus attention                     | Does anything feel cramped?             |
| Rhythm       | Pattern and pacing across elements/pages             | Is there variety within a system?       |
| Proportion   | Relative scale, ratios (golden, thirds, modular)     | Do sizes relate to each other logically?|
| Unity        | Parts feel like one whole                            | Does every element serve the idea?      |
| Simplicity   | Remove until removal hurts                           | What can be deleted without loss?       |

## Gestalt laws
Proximity, similarity, continuity, closure, figure/ground, common region, focal point.

## Layout aids
- **Rule of thirds**, **golden ratio (1:1.618)**, modular scales (1.2, 1.25, 1.333, 1.5).
- **Z and F reading patterns** for scanning layouts.

## Decision sequence
Message, audience, hierarchy, layout, type, color, imagery, detail, review.
